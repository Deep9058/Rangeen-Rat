A Dandiya Night Celebration

Nine nights of Navratri deserve one unforgettable evening of colour, dhol and dandiya. Come dressed in your favourite traditional or fusion look and celebrate with us.

DateSaturday, 17 Oct 2026
Time7:00 PM – 12:00 AM
VenueThe Grand Lawns, Fatehabad Road, Agra

The experience

Colour. Rhythm. Celebration.

Everything you need for a lively Navratri evening — from the first dhol beat to the final dandiya round.

🥁

Live Dhol & Garba Circle

A live dhol ensemble sets the beat while the garba circle grows through the evening. Join whenever you're ready.

🪄

Traditional & Fusion Dandiya

Rounds move from classic Gujarati raas to fusion Bollywood dandiya, with room on the floor for every skill level.

🍢

Food Stalls & Best-Dressed Prizes

Street-food stalls stay open all night, with prizes for the best traditional and fusion outfits.

Event flow

How the evening unfolds

Plan your night around the main dandiya rounds and performances.

7:00 PM

Gates open & welcome tilak

Entry, wristbands and a welcome tikka at the door. Dandiya sticks are available to buy if you didn't bring your own.

7:45 PM

Garba warm-up circle

A gentle warm-up circle for newcomers, led by instructors before the main floor opens.

8:30 PM

Live dhol performance

The dhol ensemble takes the stage to open the main dandiya raas.

9:00 PM

Dandiya Raas — main floor

Non-stop rounds of traditional and fusion dandiya across the full floor.

11:30 PM

Best-dressed announcement

Prizes for best traditional look, best fusion look and best couple outfit.

12:00 AM

Evening closes

Gates close. Thank you for celebrating with us — see you next Navratri!
